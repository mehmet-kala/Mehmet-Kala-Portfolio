The Project

OVERVIEW

Campus life was scattered across a dozen separate systems — class schedules, assignment uploads, cafeteria menus, ring bus times, card balance, health centre queues — each with its own login and its own page.

METU App consolidates them into a single hub with one customised home page per user type: student, staff, instructor, visitor. After the course ended, the Rectorate and a Computer Engineering student team picked the design up to develop and replace the existing METU mobile app.

Research & Groundwork

INTERVIEWS → PERSONAS

Forty-two campus users across five groups — 15 students, 11 technopolis staff, 8 staff, 5 visitors and 4 instructors — condensed into three working personas: Onur, Mahmut and Sibel.

WHAT CAME BACK

Different groups broke on different things: a first-year could not find class locations or ring schedules, a secretary had no way to reach active ring data or staff contacts, an instructor could not check parking or vegan meal options. The common thread was information that existed but was unreachable in the moment.
